Four routes for the Affswap mark

Brief: a premium, trustworthy network where verified iGaming affiliate managers swap contacts — they don't buy them. So the mark should read two-way exchange, feel clean and fintech-modern (not gambling-flashy), and hold up tiny as an app icon. Palette + type are locked: pale blue + Manrope. Same swap symbol drawn four different ways.

Recommended · clearest read
Concept 01

Exchange

The literal swap — two arrows trading places. Instantly legible at any size, universally understood as "exchange", and it's the most honest expression of the product. Confident, simple, ownable.

Concept 02

Orbit

Two arrows in a continuous loop — the swap that keeps going. Suggests an active, circulating network and "always-on" liquidity of contacts. Slightly more abstract and techy; feels like a platform, not a button.

Concept 03

Converge

Two chevrons pointing toward each other — two managers meeting in the middle to trade. The two tones say "two sides, one deal." Distinctive, a little more crafted, and it doubles as a subtle "A" for Affswap.

Concept 04

Signature

Wordmark-led: lowercase affswap with a two-way "swap swoosh" underlining it. Modern, editorial, and the most flexible for a young brand — the word is the logo. App icon uses an a·s monogram.
